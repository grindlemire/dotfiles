# Joel's voice

How Joel writes prose, for any draft or rewrite in his name: posts, docs,
marketing copy, a polished sentence. `slop.md` lists what he has flagged;
this file is what he writes instead. Where the humanizer disagrees with this
file, this file wins.

## Sentences

- **First person for opinions.** Observations are attributed to what Joel
  has seen or heard ("From the engineers I talk to…"), so contested claims
  read as reports, not demands for agreement.
- **Sentences carry their reason.** Medium length, joined by "because" and
  "since", with commas and periods doing the joining.
- **Conversational pivots.** A turn opens with "However," or "That said,".
- **Concrete specifics.** The command, the risk, the number Joel gave you.
  Incidental vendors stay generic ("internal docs").
- **"We" for walkthrough actions** ("we render the card", "we add a
  listener"); "you" only for the literal reader. The app's end user is "the
  user" or a named person.

## Kept despite the humanizer

- **A question that opens a section** by asking what the reader is
  wondering, answered by the next sentence in full (never a "Because…"
  fragment), despite humanizer §33.
- **Bold run-in leads** on list items and on a system's parts, despite §15.
- **Third person in scenes.** §26 and §27 suggest "you" to put the reader in
  the scene; Joel's openings don't.
